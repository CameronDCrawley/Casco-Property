## Casco Bay Property Care
Your yard. Your driveway. Handled. Professional year-round property maintenance providing mowing all summer and plowing all winter with one dependable crew.

## Live Demo
Netlify Deployment: https://casco-prop.netlify.app

## Features
Header Navigation: Clean brand title with quick-access links to Services, Reviews, and a Free Quote button.

Hero Section: Bold headline and supporting tagline paired with a dedicated yard work visual asset.

Comprehensive Services: Detailed breakdowns covering Mowing & Cleanups, Snow Plowing & Sanding, and Gutters & Odd Jobs.

Customer Testimonials: Real feedback from local neighbors highlighting prompt snow removal and reliable property care.

Interactive Quote Request Form: User-friendly form including fields for name, phone number, service selection dropdown, property details, and submission button.

Professional Footer: Displays licensing & insurance details, service areas (Portland, South Portland, & Cape Elizabeth), and direct contact phone number ((207) 555-0142).

## How It Works
Structure & Layout: Built using semantic HTML5 elements (<header>, <main>, <section>, <blockquote>, <footer>) to maintain clean page architecture.

Styling Integration: Linked to an external stylesheet (css/style.css) for layout structuring, container grouping, and typography.

Form Submissions: Uses interactive form elements (<textarea>, <select>, <option>, and <button>) to gather client property requests seamlessly.
